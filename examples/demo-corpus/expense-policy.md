# Northstar Works expense policy

## Scope

This policy covers business expenses incurred by Northstar Works employees while carrying out approved work activities.

## Submission deadline

Submit an expense report in the Finance Portal no later than the **fifth business day of the month following the expense**. Attach a legible receipt for every individual expense of EUR 25 or more.

## Travel and meals

Economy travel is the default for rail and air journeys. Hotel stays require manager approval before booking. Client meals require the names of attendees and a business purpose in the expense report.

## Reimbursement

Finance reviews complete reports every Friday. Approved reports are reimbursed in the next regular payroll run. Incomplete reports are returned to the employee with a request for correction.
