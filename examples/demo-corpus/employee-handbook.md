# Northstar Works employee handbook

## Purpose

Northstar Works is a fictional company used only for the Ermes Knowledge demo. This handbook describes internal practices for employees based in Italy.

## Annual leave

Employees request annual leave through the People Portal. Submit the request at least **five working days** before the first requested day. The direct manager reviews the request within two working days. Urgent exceptions require written approval from the department lead.

## Remote work

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to **two days per week**. The remote-work schedule must be agreed with the direct manager before the start of the week. Employees must use company-managed devices and must not print confidential documents outside an approved office.

## Learning budget

Each eligible employee has an annual learning budget of **EUR 750**. A manager must approve the course before purchase. The employee uploads the invoice and proof of completion to the People Portal within thirty days of the course end date.
