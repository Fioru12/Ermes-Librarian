# Northstar Works IT access policy

## Access requests

Managers request access to business systems through the Service Desk. The request must name the employee, system, business reason and required access level. The system owner approves access before IT provisions it.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the Finance Portal, the People Portal and the company VPN. Employees must report a lost authentication device to the Service Desk immediately.

## Offboarding

Human Resources starts an offboarding ticket on the employee's final working day. IT disables interactive accounts by 18:00 local time on that day. Managers are responsible for transferring business files and shared mailbox ownership before the final day.
