# Demo questions and expected evidence

Ask these questions after uploading the corpus to one library.

| Question | Expected source | Expected answer characteristic |
|---|---|---|
| How much notice is required for annual leave? | Employee handbook / Annual leave | At least five working days. |
| How many remote-work days are allowed? | Employee handbook / Remote work | Up to two days per week. |
| When is an expense report due? | Expense policy / Submission deadline | By the fifth business day of the following month. |
| When are approved expenses reimbursed? | Expense policy / Reimbursement | In the next regular payroll run. |
| Who approves access before IT provisions it? | IT access policy / Access requests | The system owner. |
| What happens to accounts on the final working day? | IT access policy / Offboarding | IT disables interactive accounts by 18:00 local time. |

Also ask: `What is the parental leave policy?` The correct product behaviour is an abstention, because the demo library contains no supporting source.
