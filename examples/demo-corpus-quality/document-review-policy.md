# Document Review Policy

Meridian Precision Works — Quality Management System

## Review Cycle

Every controlled procedure is reviewed at least once every eighteen months,
or sooner if a process change, audit finding, or customer complaint
requires it. The document owner initiates the review and records the
outcome even when no changes are made.

## Approval

A revised procedure is not published until the quality manager approves
it. Approval is recorded with a name, date, and revision number on the
document's first page before the new version replaces the previous one in
the controlled register.

## Training on Changes

When a procedure changes in a way that affects daily work, the document
owner arranges training for the affected team before the new revision
takes effect, and records attendance in the training log.
