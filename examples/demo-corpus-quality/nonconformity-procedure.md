# Nonconformity Handling Procedure

Meridian Precision Works — Quality Management System

## Reporting

Any nonconformity identified during production or inspection must be logged
in the quality register within one working day of detection. The report
must include the batch number, the affected quantity, and the process step
where the nonconformity was found.

## Containment

The quality lead places the affected batch on hold and notifies the
production supervisor before any further processing continues. Held
material is physically segregated and tagged until a disposition decision
is recorded.

## Root Cause and Disposition

A root cause analysis is required for any nonconformity affecting more than
five units. The quality lead approves the disposition — rework, scrap, or
use-as-is with customer concession — before the batch is released.
